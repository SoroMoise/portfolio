## Copy

**No em dash (U+2014) or en dash (U+2013)**, in copy, comments or docs: they read as machine-written text, and neither is ordinary punctuation in French or English. Write a period, comma, colon or parentheses instead, never a spaced hyphen in their place; a range is written the way its language does (`2024-2026`, `Depuis 2023` / `Since 2023` for an ongoing role, `de 70 à 80`, `70 to 80`).
